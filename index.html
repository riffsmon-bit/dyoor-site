Lore

DYOOR came out of trench culture translated into character design: overclocked collectors, relentless builders, and market survivors shaped by volatility, repetition, and conviction. The art carries that identity, but the goal is not just aesthetic nostalgia. Each DYOOR sits inside a broader system of products, roles, and collection utility that keeps the world active.
