FAQ / Explainer

Why are there multiple wallet confirmations?

First-time ascension may require an approval transaction. After that, your NFT is deposited into the vault and then registered on-chain so energy generation begins.

What happens if a transaction stops halfway?

Your NFT may be deposited but not yet registered. The recovery panel on this page will detect that state and let you complete registration.

What does the battery mean?

The battery reflects the share of your visible DYOOR that are currently ascended. A full battery means everything you currently hold is ascended.

What is Energy?

Each ascended DYOOR generates 24 Energy per day. Energy accrues on-chain and aligns your NFTs for future Season 2 mechanics.
