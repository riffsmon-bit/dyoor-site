DEX Router

DYOOR Swap uses Kuru Flow routing to aggregate Monad liquidity from the homepage.

Users can optionally enable the Support DYOOR route.

When enabled:

  • A transparent referrer fee is applied through Kuru Flow when supported

Support routing is optional. When enabled, part of each swap feeds the treasury that funds future product, collection utility, and community work.

Experimental onchain treasury strategy.

The D.Y.O.O.R treasury is designed to support long-term project growth, ecosystem participation, liquidity strategies, yield opportunities, partnerships, community rewards, and future utility. This is not a promise of profit, passive income, or financial return. Treasury usage is an experimental onchain strategy that may evolve as market conditions, tooling, partnerships, and community priorities change.
