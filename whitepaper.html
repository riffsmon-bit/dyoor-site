DYOOR is a Monad-native collection built around live product surfaces, treasury-backed utility, and a holder base that can direct where the project goes next. The goal is to move beyond static profile-picture status and into repeat use across the collection experience.

The operating model is simple: grow the treasury, ship product around the collection, and let holders decide what gets prioritized next.

About DYOOR NFT

DYOOR does not rely on a native token.

There is no emissions schedule, inflation loop, or token launch dependency underneath the collection.

The project is built as an NFT-first ecosystem with treasury-backed product mechanics rather than token-first speculation.

Revenue sources:

  • DEX Router: 75% of Support DYOOR swap fees go directly into the Community Fund.
  • Secondary Royalties: 75% go directly into the Community Fund.

Treasury allocation and future utility direction are decided through holder governance.

DEX Router

DYOOR Swap uses a PancakeSwap-based routing layer to keep trading familiar while giving the project its own branded shell on top.

Users can optionally enable the Support DYOOR route.

When enabled:

  • An additional fee is applied
  • 75% of that fee goes directly to the Community Fund (Treasury)

Support routing is optional. When enabled, part of each swap feeds the treasury that funds future product, collection utility, and community work.
